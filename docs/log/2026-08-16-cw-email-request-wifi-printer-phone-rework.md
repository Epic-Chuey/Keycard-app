# 2026-08-16 — CW Email Request: Wi-Fi/Printer field reorder + relaxed validation, Phone rebuilt bespoke

Batch of changes across three tabs. **Wi-Fi**: Office or Warehouse moved above Company Name, "Near AP / Node" field removed, Email/Phone made unconditionally optional (fields kept), a free-text Notes box added (survives De-activate's field-hiding since it sits right after Company/Tenant Name), and Troubleshoot entries now require nothing (Create/De-activate unchanged). **Printer**: Office or Warehouse moved above Company Name, a Notes box added under Phone, Troubleshoot entries require nothing (Setup New Printer keeps its required fields). **Phone**: rebuilt off the shared `ERA_SIMPLE_MODES` template entirely into a bespoke shape (mirrors Keycard) via new `eraCreatePhoneEntry()`/`eraApplyPhoneModeVisibility()` — New Phone Line gets Location (autocomplete), Temp Agent/Direct Hire, Person First/Last Name, Person Title (dropdown), Person Responsibilities, Report to Manager (email), no attachment; Troubleshoot gets Location, Phone Tag Number, User First/Last Name, User's Personal Phone Number, "Describe the issue" (per-entry now, page-level `era_ph_issue` removed), plus an optional attachment with a photo tip (only shown under Troubleshoot). Server-side: `buildWifi()`'s required list drops to `[]` under Troubleshoot; `GENERIC_CONFIG`/`buildGeneric()` gained a `relaxTroubleshoot` flag (Printer only); Phone moved off `buildGeneric()` into a new `buildPhone(p)` with its own validation/body-building. One assumption made without an explicit spec: "Location Features" was implemented as a plain autocomplete Location text field, not a distinct "features" concept — flag to Huy if wrong.
